The agent builds from this file. Keep each section short and concrete.

## Premise

What the game is, in a few sentences.

## Content

What lives in `data/` and `maps/`: item, actor, dialogue and encounter files, and their fields.

The systems in `rules/`: combat, inventory, progression, saves. For each, the inputs, the outputs and the edge cases worth a test.

## Shell

The screens and scenes in `shell/`, and what each one shows.
